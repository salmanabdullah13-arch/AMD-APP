# Design scorecard — findings (5 Sep 2026)

The measured table is `design-scorecard.md` (regenerated by
`design-scorecard.js`); the screenshots are in `test-run/scorecard/`. This
file is the part a script cannot grade: what the screenshots show against
the checklist drawn from the design packages (shell non-negotiables, stat
strip, label-above-bar, scroll clearance, empty-state copy, status
vocabulary, date and currency format, badge tones, the page template, the
money rule per role, and the "first five seconds" test per role).

## Measured: 28 screens, 4 flagged — all four the Curtain granular dashboards (no shell, no dark), which are item 4; the Operations overflow is fixed

## The outliers, and what to do about each

**Curtain & Blinds and its four granular dashboards (Tracks, QC, Site
Installer, Team Leader)** — the only screens with no shell chrome and no
dark mode. They are the pre-shell `position:fixed` overlays from 3 Aug,
with their own wine header and a "← Back" button. Clean, legible, and
clearly from a different generation than every other screen a person
now moves between. **This is item 4 in Salman's order (Curtain restyle
into the shared shell, no rebuild)** — the scorecard confirms it is the
right next piece and that it is the last family of screens outside the
shell.

**Storekeeper** — lands on the legacy stock-pool dashboard: seven KPI
tiles (the seventh alone on its row), a "Stock Movement" chart of
near-zero counts, and the In-Pool list. **The 18a Store Keeper package's
interface was never built** — only its data layer (`store-data.js`, 19
Aug: locations, bins, holds, issues, transfers, counts, all live on the
project with server-side gates). The storekeeper today cannot reach any
of that from a screen. The largest single gap the scorecard found; a
build of the same shape as 17a and 20a (one shell, twelve pages, seven
flows), not a restyle.

**Approver** — six count tiles and a "Category Breakdown" chart of three
zeros filling the landing. The Approver's actual work (quotations
awaiting approval) is one nav item away and appears on the landing only
as a count. Fails the first-five-seconds test: the person who signs in
here needs the queue in front of them, with the aging badge the 6 Aug
audit added, not a category histogram. A small rework, not a package.

**Operations at 390px** — 5px of horizontal overflow, traced to the bell's
pulse ring scaling past the topbar edge (`.xs-pulse` at 1.45×). A shell
defect, so it affected every module's topbar, flagged first on
Operations. Fixed: the topbar row clips horizontally.

**A harness artifact, not a defect** — several phone screenshots showed
the sidebar drawer half-open and cut off at the left. The screen was
opened at 1440 and resized to 390, and the shot caught the sidebar
mid-slide to its drawer position. A settle wait after resizing removed
it; a direct open at 390 shows the drawer closed at −273px.

## Smaller consistency notes (no package broken, worth a pass later)

- **Currency format.** Accounts' tiles print `BD 1408.407`; the design
  packages and the print documents use `BD 1,408.407`. The shared
  `cwFmt`/dashboard formatters already produce the grouped form —
  Accounts' KPI band predates them.
- **Categorical colours on mini-bar charts.** HR's Compliance Risk chart
  colours its six bars blue, green, amber and wine; the packages keep
  one accent and carry meaning in the label and the tone badge. Fleet
  Health does the same with red for "failed". Semantic red/amber for a
  bad/warn state is defensible; six unrelated hues are decoration.
- **Empty charts.** Approver's Category Breakdown and Storekeeper's Stock
  Movement render three hairline dashes for zero data. `cwMiniBars` has
  an empty state; these two call it with zeros rather than letting it
  say "not enough data yet".
- **Jobs** keeps the pre-package list layout (filter card, list rows) —
  coherent with the shell and readable, but without a stat strip or page
  template. Acceptable as a working list; not a dashboard.

## What passed cleanly

Every module built from a design package — Owner, Sales, Estimator,
Operations, Purchase, Production (all four role landings), Upholstery
(all three), Admin, Crew clock, Delivery/Scheduling, Fleet, HR, Accounts,
Jobs — carries the shell, holds at 390px, goes genuinely dark by computed
style, keeps every chart label out of its fill, and shows the money rule
holding on the five role landings that must never see a figure.
