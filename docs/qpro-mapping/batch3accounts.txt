# Q-Pro Jobs — Batch 3: Accounts Module (Functional Spec)

## Scope covered
Transactions → Accounts (General Receipt, General Payment, Journal) and their supporting
Masters → Accounts (Accounts Group, Ledger, Customer accounting fields, Vendor — already
covered in Batch 1). This batch defines the general-ledger / bookkeeping layer that sits
underneath Sales and Purchases.

---

## 1. Chart of Accounts structure (Masters → Accounts → Accounts Group)

Classic two-level Tally-style chart of accounts.

**15 system "Primary" groups** (root-level, not editable, no parent):
Branch/Divisions, Capital Account, Current Assets, Current Liabilities, Direct Expenses,
Direct Incomes, Fixed Assets, Indirect Expenses, Indirect Incomes, Investments,
Loans (Liability), Misc. Expenses (Asset), Purchase Accounts, Sales Accounts, Suspense A/c.

**11 nested/custom sub-groups**, each assigned to one of the primaries, e.g.:
Customers → Current Assets, Suppliers → Current Liabilities, Sales → Current Assets,
Duties & Taxes → Current Liabilities, Purchases → Direct Expenses, Cash Accounts →
Current Assets, Bank Accounts → Current Assets, Salary & Staff Costs / Staff Salaries /
Machinery Repair & Maintenance / Tools & Equipment → Indirect Expenses.

Only the 11 custom sub-groups have an **Edit** action; the 15 system primaries are locked.

**Add Accounts Group form fields:**
- Group Name*
- Under* — dropdown of all 15 primary groups (or "Primary" itself, to create a new
  top-level group)
- Primary Group* — fundamental classification: **Asset / Liability / Income / Expense**
  (drives which financial statement — Balance Sheet vs P&L — the group lands on)
- Sort Option (numeric, controls display order in reports)

## 2. Ledger master (Masters → Accounts → Ledger)

This is the actual Chart-of-Accounts ledger list — the same data source used by every
autocomplete "Ledger" field across General Receipt, General Payment, and Journal.

List columns: Ledger Code (auto, `LED00xx`), Ledger Name, Accounts Group, Edit.

Notable structural finding: **individual fixed assets are tracked as their own ledger
accounts.** E.g. specific machines/tools appear as named ledgers (with model/serial info
embedded in the name) grouped under "Machinery Repair & Maintenance" or "Tools &
Equipment" — the Ledger master doubles as a light asset register rather than having a
separate Fixed Asset module.

**Add Ledger form fields:**
- Ledger Code (auto-numbered, locked)
- Ledger Name*
- Under (Group)* — autocomplete against Accounts Group master (shows Group Name / Primary
  parent / Asset-Liability-Income-Expense classification in the suggestion dropdown)
- Taxability* — dropdown: Taxable (10%), Taxable (5%), Taxable (0%), Exempt (0%),
  Out of scope (0%) (Bahrain VAT categories)
- Bank Account Number, Bank Account Holder Name, IBAN Number, Bank Swift, Bank Name,
  Bank Branch — present on every ledger (used when the ledger represents a bank account,
  e.g. under "Bank Accounts" group; left blank for non-bank ledgers)
- Opening Balance
- Is Payroll (checkbox — flags ledgers used in payroll postings)

There is **no separate "Bank" master** — bank accounts are simply Ledger records under the
"Bank Accounts" group with the banking fields populated.

## 3. Customer master — accounting-side fields (Masters → Accounts → Customer)

Same underlying customer record as the one created/edited from the Enquiry flow (confirmed
by opening our ZZTEST customer here), but this view surfaces full accounting fields not
shown on the Enquiry-side quick-create form:
- Name*, Contact Person*, Telephone*, Telephone 2, Email, Fax
- Vat Name, Vat No, Tax % (dropdown, defaults 10%)
- Is Credit (checkbox — toggles credit-sale eligibility)
- Credit Limit, Credit Days
- Bank Account Number, Bank Account Holder Name, IBAN, Swift, Bank Name, Bank Branch
  (customer's own bank details, e.g. for refunds)
- Address*, Country (dropdown, default Bahrain), Sales Man (dropdown)
- Opening Balance (read-only display, e.g. "0.000 DR")
- Status (dropdown: Approve / …)
- CR No (Commercial Registration number)
- **Openings sub-table**: Bill No / Bill Date / Bill Amount / O/S Amount / Bill Type
  (dropdown, "Invoice") / S.Man / + row — lets you load a customer's pre-existing
  outstanding invoices when onboarding them mid-cycle, so their opening receivable balance
  is correct from day one.

Vendor master fields already documented in Batch 1 (mirrors this closely on the payables side).

## 4. General Receipt (Transactions → Accounts → General Receipt)

**Purpose:** records money received from any source and codes it straight to one or more
General Ledger accounts. Confirmed this is **not** tied to a specific customer or invoice —
the ledger autocomplete only matches Chart-of-Accounts entries (Sales, Sales Return,
Discount (Sales/Purchase), VAT, Printing & Stationery, Freight & Courier Charges, Salary,
Air Ticket/Air Ticket Payable, Purchase, etc.) and returns "No Results Found" for a
customer/supplier name — confirmed by testing "ZZTEST" (no match).

List columns: Receipt Number, Receipt Date, Receipt Amount, Action. (List was empty —
no General Receipts exist yet in this company's data.)

**Create form fields:**
- Date (auto, editable)
- Five parallel payment-mode blocks, each with an amount field and a checkbox
  (checkbox likely toggles a hidden ledger-mapping selector for that mode):
  - **Cash** — Amt (maps to a "Cash" ledger)
  - **Bank** — Amt + Bank (free-text bank name); ledger options: Bank Transfer, BBK Bank,
    Benefit Pay, Cheque, Credit Card
  - **C Card** — Amt + Type (Visa Card / Master Card / Others) + Authorized
  - **Wallet** — Amt + Type (B wallet / IBAN / Benefit) + Authorized
  - **Cheque** — Amt + Number + Bank
- **Amount** (required, green highlight) — **auto-calculated as the sum of all five
  payment-mode amounts**; confirmed live (entering 50 in Cash auto-populated Amount as
  50.000)
- Line-item allocation table: **# / Ledger (autocomplete, GL accounts only) / Amount /
  Narration** + row add button, with a Total row
- Remarks (free text)
- Create Receipt / Cancel

Validation confirmed live: submitting with Amount unset/mismatched triggers "Please check
entered Amount."

## 5. General Payment (Transactions → Accounts → General Payment)

**Purpose:** structurally identical header (same 5 payment-mode blocks, same auto-summed
Amount field) to General Receipt, but its **line-item table is richer and can be tied to a
specific Job** — a genuine functional difference from General Receipt.

List columns: Payment Number (`GPyy0000n` format, e.g. `GP2500001` — no `-AMD` suffix
unlike Sales/Purchases documents), Payment Date, Payment Amount, Action. Cancelled records
are highlighted in red/pink and their **Edit action is disabled** (confirmed: clicking Edit
on a cancelled row is a no-op).

**Line-item table columns:** # / Ledger (GL autocomplete) / Amount / Narration /
**Job No** / **Job Item** (with a 🔗 link icon) / row delete.

Clicking the 🔗 icon opens a **"Search Job Item"** modal: Group (dropdown) / Sub Group
(dropdown) / Item Name (search box) / Cancel / Search — lets you tie the payment line to a
specific line-item within a specific job.

**Real example found (GP2500001, cancelled):** used to refund a customer's advance payment,
ledger "Sales Return", amount 684.000, linked to a real Job No and a job item ("Fabrication
of Button" type item), with a Remarks note explaining the job was cancelled due to a
material/fabric change requested by the client. This confirms General Payment's primary
real-world use case is refunding customer advances / miscellaneous outgoing payments tied
back to a job, as opposed to Journal (pure GL) or the Purchases-module Payment screen
(supplier-invoice settlement, documented in Batch 1).

## 6. Journal (Transactions → Accounts → Journal)

**Purpose:** free-form double-entry voucher — the most flexible of the three Accounts
transaction types, supporting multiple debit and credit lines in a single entry.

List columns: JL Number (`JLyy0000n`, e.g. `JL2600100`), Date, Debit Amount, Credit Amount,
Action (Print / Edit / Cancel). Every entry's Debit Amount total equals its Credit Amount
total (enforced double-entry balance) — confirmed across ~100 real entries in the list.

**Create/Edit form:**
- J No (auto), Date (auto, editable)
- Line table, minimum 2 rows by default (row 1 = Debit-side, row 2 = Credit-side, indicated
  by a small DR/CR toggle icon in the leftmost column), extendable via an **"+ Add a new
  Row"** button (visible on Edit view; each row independently togglable as DR or CR)
- Columns per row: **Ledger** (GL autocomplete) / **DR** / **CR** / **Rev Ledger**
  (reversal/contra ledger — likely used for automated reversing entries) / Narration /
  **Job No** (searchable by job number or client name) / **Job Item** (with the same 🔗
  Search Job Item modal as General Payment)
- Total row (Dr total / Cr total, must balance)
- Remarks
- Create Journal / Update Journal / Cancel

**Real example found (JL2600100):** a 3-line journal recording a sales commission payment —
two debit lines to a "Project Cost - Commission" ledger (100.560 + 100.000) each tied to the
same real Job No with narration "Commission 10%," offset by one credit line of 200.560 to
"BBK Bank," with a Remarks note that the commission was paid to a staff member/agent by EFT
(individual's name withheld from this spec per PII policy). This is a clean real-world
illustration of how Job-level cost allocation flows through the general ledger.

## 7. Cross-module design pattern confirmed this batch

- **General Receipt** = pure GL-coded receipt, no Job/Customer linkage.
- **General Payment** = GL-coded payment, optional Job No + Job Item linkage (for
  job-related refunds/adjustments).
- **Journal** = fully flexible multi-line double entry, optional Job No + Job Item linkage
  on every line, supports N debit/credit lines rather than a fixed single total.
- All three share the identical 5-mode payment breakdown header (Cash/Bank/C Card/
  Wallet/Cheque) with an auto-summed Amount, and all three draw their "Ledger" autocomplete
  from the same Masters → Accounts → Ledger chart of accounts.
- Numbering convention differs from Sales/Purchases documents: Accounts vouchers use
  `XXyy00000n` (e.g. `GP2500001`, `JL2600100`) with no `-AMD` company suffix, whereas
  Sales/Purchases documents use `XX-nnnn-AMD`.
